OldPicFix Privacy Policy

App: OldPicFix / 照片修复大师. Android package: vip.wangdoudou.oldpicfix. Updated October 1, 2026. The effective date will be stated in the public release.

Provider and contact

Provider: wangdoudou. Google Play developer: wjkkal; public developer name: wangjin. For privacy, deletion or appeals: wangjin8909@163.com or in-app feedback. We do not operate an automated email delivery service.

Preconfigured app review access uses a separate username and password. The server stores a password hash; the app stores only the session, not the review password. This identity is separate from Google accounts. Disabling or deleting it revokes access.

Data and purposes

  • Google subject identifier, verified email and basic profile: authentication and account ownership. We do not collect your Google password.
  • Selected photos, restoration instructions, source and result files, task states and timestamps: uploading, AI restoration, comparison, downloads and troubleshooting. We do not read your entire photo library. Upload processing removes unnecessary metadata such as location while retaining orientation and color information.
  • Point balances and entries, purchase tokens, products, verified order and refund states, and necessary referral links: delivery, consumption, reconciliation and abuse prevention. Google Play handles payments; we do not receive full payment-card details.
  • Installation identifier, device model, Android/app version, language/region, access IP, country and city inferred from that IP, and necessary access/error records: compatibility, account security and operations. Runtime logs record sign-in, session restoration and observed key operations. IP-based region estimates can be inaccurate and are not precise GPS locations. Logs avoid credentials, photo bodies and payment tokens.
  • FCM tokens, permission/language and notification states: restoration and expiring-point notifications. System notifications are optional. Firebase Analytics and advertising SDKs are currently disabled.
  • Voluntary feedback, optional contact details, result reports, moderation notes and administrator audit: support, content safety and appeals. Moderators view the reported result only when necessary; we do not create extra photo evidence copies.

Third parties

Google provides identity verification, Play purchase validation and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Our server forwards photos and restoration instructions through RightAPI to upstream AI services for processing. According to the integration information currently available to us, RightAPI acts only as a relay and does not retain these data. Actual upstream processing and retention/deletion still require verification before public paid restoration opens. To look up the approximate region of an access IP, our server may send that IP to ip-api.com, ipapi.co, ip.sb or the Taobao IP lookup service. These requests do not include photos or restoration instructions. The lookup services' retention practices require verification and disclosure before public release. We do not sell photos or account data. Legally required disclosures are limited to permitted, necessary purposes.

Retention and deletion

Photos on this app’s servers are kept for no more than 30 days: source photos from upload and result photos from generation. This includes unsubmitted, failed and unlinked photo files. Restoring a photo again does not extend the original file deadline. Access ends and automatic cleanup removes expired files; you can delete photos or your account earlier. A shorter period may apply; check the photo record and save results promptly. This rule does not cover copies saved on your device. Upstream AI data handling is verified and disclosed separately.

Delete in Settings and Help, or use the web deletion page without reinstalling. Verify the same Google account and confirm deletion. We revoke sessions, push tokens and photo access, remove identifiable photo files and account identities/profile details, and hide tasks from user history. Pending file cleanup can be retried; contact support if needed. Saved device copies remain under your control.

Minimum transaction, point and deletion/security audit records are retained for applicable regional legal obligations and necessary purposes, then cleaned when these expire. Specific periods require operator and release-region verification. A purpose-separated irreversible anti-abuse fingerprint is retained for 180 days to prevent repeat registration/referral rewards. Web credentials are not written to logs or business tables; deletion authorization expires after 10 minutes.

Contact us for access, correction, deletion, objections or appeals; ownership verification protects other users. Deleting this account does not delete your Google account or automatically refund Play purchases.

Security, children and changes

Connections between the app and our server use HTTPS, account authorization, private photo storage and audited administrator access. The server currently uses HTTP when sending access IPs to ip-api.com for region lookup, so we cannot state that every transmission of all collected data is encrypted. Server photo files currently have no additional encryption at rest; no system is absolutely secure. Upload only authorized photos and no illegal or prohibited content, including child sexual exploitation. The app is designed primarily for older users and is not directed to children. Material purpose changes will be disclosed. Provider processing details and applicable retention periods must be completed before publication.

我们处理的数据与目的

  • Google账号主体标识、已验证邮箱、昵称和账号基本资料：用于验证身份、建立账号和区分数据归属。我们不收集Google密码。
  • 预置的应用审核用户名及独立密码：仅用于授权审核访问；服务器保存密码散列，客户端只保存登录会话，不保存审核密码。该身份与Google账号分开，停用或删除后撤销访问。
  • 您主动选择的照片、补充修复要求、原图与修复结果、任务状态和时间：用于上传、AI修复、进度查询、对比、下载及故障处理。不会读取整个相册；上传处理去除定位等非必要元数据并保留方向和色彩信息。
  • 积分批次和流水、商品与购买凭证、Google核验状态、退款撤销和必要的邀请关联：用于发放、消费、退款核对、防止重复发货和奖励滥用。付款由Google Play处理，我们不接收完整银行卡资料。
  • 安装标识、机型、Android与应用版本、语言地区、访问IP及由IP推算的国家和城市、必要的访问和错误记录：用于兼容性诊断、账号安全和运营核对。运行日志记录登录、恢复会话和已观察到的关键操作；由IP推算的地域可能不准确，不代表GPS精确位置。日志避免包含认证凭据、照片正文和支付令牌。
  • 您允许通知时的FCM令牌、通知权限与语言，以及应用内消息状态：用于修复完成和积分到期通知；拒绝系统通知不影响修复。当前不启用Firebase Analytics或广告SDK。
  • 自愿提交的反馈、可选联系方式、结果举报、处理说明与管理员审计：用于客服、内容安全和处理申诉。管理员仅在处理举报所需范围查看对应结果；不额外复制照片作为举报证据。

保存、删除与账号权利

应用内“设置与帮助→注销账号”或网页删除入口均可提出删除，无需重新安装应用。网页要求验证同一Google账号并再次确认。删除会撤销登录会话、通知令牌和照片访问，清理可定位照片文件，删除账号身份及个人资料；任务从用户历史隐藏。文件清理未完成的网页请求显示待清理，可重试并联系支持。设备相册内已保存的副本由您自行管理。

删除后仍按发行地区法定要求及必要目的保留最少的交易、积分和删除／安全审计，目的结束且适用期限届满后清理；具体期限须在运营主体和发行地区核实后确定，不承诺任意年数。为防止重复奖励，保留180天用途隔离、不可逆的账号指纹，期间重新注册不再发放注册或邀请奖励。网页验证临时凭证不保存到日志或交易表；短时删除授权10分钟到期。

安全、儿童与变更

APP与本应用服务器之间使用HTTPS、账号鉴权和私有照片存储，限制管理员访问并记录操作。当前服务器向ip-api.com查询访问IP地域时使用HTTP，因此不能承诺所有数据的全部传输环节均已加密。服务器照片文件目前不作额外静态加密，不承诺绝对安全。请仅上传有权处理的照片，不提交非法、儿童性剥削或其他受禁止内容。本应用主要面向大龄用户，不面向儿童；相关情况请联系支持。重大处理用途变更会在政策和应用中告知，正式版本将补齐供应商处理说明及适用保留期限。
