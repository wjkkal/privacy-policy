LinkLink Classic — HarmonyOS Privacy Policy

Updated: 10 October 2026. This notice describes the HarmonyOS version 1.0.39. Package: vip.wangdoudou.lianliankan. Provider: wangdoudou (王晋). Contact: wangjin8909@163.com.

1. Offline gameplay and local information

You can play without signing in or connecting to the internet. Level progress, scores, stars, unlocked themes, daily tasks, tutorial status, game settings and session records are stored in the app's private storage on your device. This HarmonyOS version does not automatically upload these records or register your device with the Android service.

An installation-scoped random identifier is stored locally for optional feedback. It is not your Android ID, advertising ID, IMEI, UDID or Huawei account identifier. Public app version and device model information may be read locally; they are not automatically sent to our server.

2. Optional feedback

When you choose Submit in Settings > Feedback, the app sends the installation identifier, the feedback text and any title or contact details you chose to enter to the developer's HTTPS service at wangdoudou.vip. A contact field is optional. The service also receives the network IP address needed to deliver the request. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in feedback.

This information is used to handle the issue and, where you supplied a contact, to reply. Submitting feedback requires a connection and can fail if the service is unavailable. Not submitting feedback does not limit offline gameplay. The request may be processed outside your country or region.

3. Huawei Game Service, advertising, purchases and permissions

This version integrates native Huawei Game Service for Huawei account sign-in and player verification. It attempts sign-in at startup. Huawei may display its game service and privacy statement and account screens when required. Please decide whether to accept in Huawei's system interface. The service may process the Huawei account authorization status, game player identifier, app information and network connection information needed for this flow under Huawei's game service and privacy statement. This app does not obtain or store your Huawei account password. Accounts outside the service's supported regions, lack of connectivity or cancellation may prevent verification; these outcomes are not recorded as successful verification.

The developer does not upload Huawei account information or game player identifiers to its own server and does not persist the player identifier. Local gameplay and growth data are not automatically uploaded or linked to the Huawei account. Switching accounts does not clear local progress. Unavailable sign-in or verification does not create an internet requirement for offline gameplay; Huawei's own account and verification screens operate under its rules.

This version has no in-app purchases. Google advertising, Google sign-in and Google Play purchase services are not initialized on HarmonyOS. No Huawei advertising service is enabled in this package. Internet access is used for Huawei Game Service and optional feedback; vibration is used for game feedback. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permission.

4. Control, retention and deletion

Local data remains in the app's private storage until you clear the app data or uninstall it; device backup and restore settings may affect this. Resetting levels only returns the mainline to level one and preserves stars, themes and completion history. For access, correction or deletion of feedback you previously sent, contact wangjin8909@163.com with enough information to identify that feedback. We use feedback for resolving support requests and retain it only as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations.

5. Children and security

Offline gameplay does not require a child's name, account or contact information. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sending personal information in optional feedback. Network feedback uses HTTPS with the operating system's certificate validation. No security measure can guarantee complete protection.

6. External pages and changes

The published copy of this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening that website makes a normal web request governed by GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. You can read this bundled policy inside Settings without opening a website. Material changes to data handling or enabled services require an updated notice; a later notice must be assessed against that later app version.

连连看经典版 — 鸿蒙版隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月10日。本声明适用于鸿蒙版本1.0.39，包名 vip.wangdoudou.lianliankan。开发者：wangdoudou（王晋）。联系邮箱：wangjin8909@163.com。

一、离线玩法与本地信息

无需登录或连接网络即可游玩。关卡进度、成绩、星星、已解锁主题、每日任务、教程状态、游戏设置及会话记录保存在设备上的应用私有目录中。本鸿蒙版本不会自动上传这些记录，也不会调用原Android服务注册设备。

应用在本地保存一个仅属于本次安装的随机标识，用于可选的意见反馈。该标识不是Android ID、广告标识、IMEI、UDID或华为账号标识。应用版本和公开机型信息可能在本机读取，不会自动发送到开发者服务器。

二、自愿提交意见反馈

当您在“设置 > 意见反馈”中选择提交时，应用向开发者的 wangdoudou.vip HTTPS服务发送安装内随机标识、反馈内容，以及您主动填写的标题和联系方式。联系方式为选填。服务器还会接收传输请求所需的网络IP地址。请勿在反馈中填写不必要的敏感信息。

这些信息用于处理问题，并在您提供联系方式时回复。提交反馈需要网络，服务不可用时可能失败；不提交反馈不影响离线玩法。该请求可能在您所在国家或地区之外处理。

三、华为游戏服务、广告、购买与权限

本版本接入原生华为游戏服务，用于华为账号登录及玩家校验。启动时会尝试登录；首次使用时，华为服务按其规则展示游戏服务与隐私声明及登录界面，请在系统界面中自行决定是否同意。服务可能处理完成该流程所需的华为账号授权状态、游戏玩家标识、应用信息及网络连接信息，由华为按其游戏服务与隐私声明处理；本应用不取得或保存您的华为账号密码。境外账号可能因华为服务的地域支持范围而无法完成校验，断网或取消登录时也可能失败，本应用不会将这些结果记录为校验成功。

开发者不会将华为账号资料或游戏玩家标识上传至自己的服务器，不持久保存该玩家标识；本地关卡和成长数据仍不自动上传，不与华为账号绑定，切换账号不会清除本地进度。登录或玩家校验不可用不会成为离线玩法的联网门槛；系统自身提供的账号或校验界面按华为规则处理。

本版本不提供应用内购买。在鸿蒙上不初始化Google广告、Google登录和Google Play购买服务，本软件包也未启用华为广告服务。网络权限用于华为游戏服务和自愿提交反馈，振动权限用于游戏触感反馈。应用不申请摄像头、麦克风、通讯录或精确位置权限。

四、控制、保留与删除

本地数据保存在应用私有目录中，您可通过系统清除应用数据或卸载应用删除；设备的备份恢复设置可能影响其恢复情况。“重置关卡”只将主线返回第一关，并保留星星、主题及完成历史。若需访问、更正或删除已经发送的反馈，请联系 wangjin8909@163.com 并提供足以识别该次反馈的信息。反馈仅用于处理支持请求，并按处理所需及适用义务保留。

五、儿童与安全

离线玩法不要求儿童提供姓名、账号或联系方式。儿童在自愿反馈中提供个人信息前，应征得家长或监护人同意。反馈请求使用HTTPS和操作系统的证书校验；任何安全措施都无法保证绝对安全。

六、外部页面与更新

本隐私政策的公开页面由GitHub Pages托管，打开网页会产生普通网络请求，适用GitHub隐私声明：https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement。您也可在设置内离线阅读随包声明，无需打开网页。若以后改变数据处理方式或启用新的服务，应更新相应声明；以后版本应以其实际能力和对应声明为准。
