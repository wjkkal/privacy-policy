DouScan 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-05（适用于Android 1.0.9及后续版本）

开发者：wjkkal

联系邮箱：wangjin8909@163.com

本政策适用于“二维码与条码扫描器 - DouScan”（原名 QR Scanner & Generator Offline）。下述新版行为在安装该版本后适用；旧版本的差异见下文。

扫码、生成与历史记录

本应用读取相机画面或你主动选择的图片，在设备本地识别二维码和条形码。生成内容与扫码历史也保存在本机，不上传到我们的服务器。新版禁用 Android 对应用数据的系统备份和设备迁移。你主动分享或导出内容时，你选择的目标应用会接收相应内容。

网络与意见反馈

扫码和生成二维码可以离线使用。你主动打开扫码链接时，浏览器会访问相应网站，受该网站隐私政策约束；应用不会自动打开链接。提交意见反馈时，我们的服务器会收到反馈内容、反馈类别、应用生成的标识，以及你自愿填写的联系方式，用于回复和处理反馈。你可以通过上述邮箱申请删除与该标识或联系方式关联的反馈。

Android 1.0.9及后续版本会在打开应用时，通过 HTTPS 向开发者服务器发送无需登录账号的启动记录：应用生成并保存在本机的随机标识、设备型号、系统与应用版本、系统地区以及启动时间。服务器同时记录请求 IP 并补充 IP 国家／地区和城市，用于运行统计与故障排查；系统地区与 IP 地域分别记录，不使用定位权限。随机标识不是 Google 账号、Android ID、广告标识符或姓名；启动记录不表示已验证账号登录。

断网时，记录保存在本机；恢复前台或下次启动后补传，收到服务器对该条事件的确认后才清除本机待办。服务器继续按既有后台日志保留与清理规则保存记录，本机删除不等于服务器同步删除。本应用不包含广告 SDK，不在启动记录中发送扫码／生成内容、相机图像、图片、历史、收藏、联系方式或反馈正文。你可通过上述邮箱申请删除与本机随机标识关联的远程记录；卸载可清除本机标识及待办。较早版本的遥测行为可能不同，本政策须与实际安装版本对应。

权限

相机权限用于实时扫码。只有当你选择图片扫码或为二维码添加 Logo 时，应用才读取所选图片。网络权限用于启动记录、主动打开链接及提交反馈；振动权限用于扫码反馈。目前 Android 构建不申请定位权限。

你的选择与安全

你可以在应用中删除本地历史，或卸载应用以移除本地数据。意见反馈是可选功能。我们服务的网络请求使用 HTTPS。我们不出售扫码内容。

变更与联系

应用行为变化时，我们可能更新本政策。如有隐私问题或删除请求，请联系 wangjin8909@163.com。

Privacy Policy — DouScan

Last updated: 2026-11-05 (Android 1.0.9 and later)

Developer: wjkkal

Contact: wangjin8909@163.com

This policy applies to QR & Barcode Scanner: DouScan (formerly QR Scanner & Generator Offline). The practices described for the upcoming release apply when that version is installed; older versions may behave differently as noted below.

Scanning, creation, and history

The app reads camera images or photos you choose to scan. QR and barcode contents are decoded on your device. Codes you create and scan history are also stored locally. They are not uploaded to our server. Android system backup and device transfer of app data are disabled in the upcoming release. When you choose to share or export content, the destination app you select receives it.

Network and feedback

Scanning and generating codes work offline. If you choose to open a scanned link, your browser contacts that website under its own privacy policy. Links do not open automatically. If you submit feedback, our server receives your message, feedback category, an app-generated identifier, and any contact details you voluntarily enter. We use these to respond to and resolve feedback. Contact us at the address above to request deletion of feedback associated with your identifier or contact details.

Android 1.0.9 and later sends a minimal startup record without requiring an account to the developer's server over HTTPS: a random identifier generated and stored by the app, device model, operating system and app versions, system region, and startup time. The server also receives the request IP address and resolves its country or region and city for operational statistics and troubleshooting. System region and IP region are kept separate; location permission is not used. The random identifier is not a Google account, Android ID, advertising ID, or name. A startup record does not mean that an account has been authenticated.

While offline, records stay on the device. The app retries when it returns to the foreground or starts again, and removes each local pending record only after the server confirms that event. Server records remain subject to the existing log retention and cleanup policy; clearing a local pending record does not delete the server record. Startup records never contain scanned or generated contents, camera images, photos, history, favorites, contact details, or feedback messages. The app contains no advertising SDK. You may email us to request deletion of remote records associated with your app-generated identifier. Uninstalling clears the local identifier and pending records. Earlier versions may behave differently; the published policy must match the version being distributed.

Permissions

Camera access is used for live scanning. Photos are accessed only when you select an image for scanning or a logo for a generated code. Internet access supports startup records, links, and voluntary feedback. Vibration provides scan feedback. The app does not request location access in the current Android build.

Your choices and security

You can delete local history in the app or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Feedback is optional. Network requests to our service use HTTPS. We do not sell scanned contents.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app behavior changes. Contact wangjin8909@163.com with privacy questions or deletion requests.
