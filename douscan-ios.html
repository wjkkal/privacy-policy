Privacy Policy — DouScan for iOS

Effective date: November 5, 2026. This policy applies to DouScan for iOS, version 1.0.8.

Scanning, generation and local records

QR & Barcode Scanner: DouScan processes camera frames and selected images on your device to decode QR codes and barcodes. Codes you generate, scan history, favorites and saved batches are processed and stored locally. These contents are not automatically uploaded to the developer. Scanning and code generation work offline. Opening the app does not automatically send device-registration or app-open telemetry.

Permissions and system actions

Camera access is used for live scanning. Photos are used when you select an image to scan, select a logo, or save generated images. Contact and calendar actions open the relevant system editor for your confirmation. Older iOS versions may request calendar access; system contact suggestions may involve contacts permission. Permissions can be managed in iOS Settings. The app does not automatically send your contacts, calendar or photo library to the developer.

Actions you choose to share externally

When you confirm opening a website, the selected browser or web view contacts that website. Search, maps and product lookup display the destination and content for confirmation: the displayed scan text is sent to Google Search, location content to Google Maps, or a product barcode to Barcode Lookup, respectively. Canceling the confirmation does not start the query. Receiving services handle information under their own privacy policies.

When you share, export CSV, or export a backup, the destination application or location you select receives the chosen contents. A destination may be a third-party service or cloud-backed folder. DouScan does not operate cloud synchronization for your scan records. Device-level backup and restoration are subject to your Apple settings; this policy does not promise that all iOS backups are disabled.

Optional feedback

If you submit feedback, the developer's server receives your message, feedback category, an app-generated installation identifier and any contact details you voluntarily enter. These are used to investigate feedback and communicate with you. Feedback uses HTTPS. Submitting feedback is optional and is separate from scanning and generation.

The backend does not retain feedback submissions or their associated installation identifiers and contact details after processing the request. There is no retained backend feedback record for you to request to delete. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information in feedback. You may contact the developer using the email below for privacy questions.

Advertising and local deletion

This iOS version has no advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking. Local records have no fixed retention period by default: they remain on your device until you delete them or enable the app's optional retention cleanup settings. You can delete local history and saved records through the app. Copies you explicitly export or share, and system backups, are controlled separately by their destinations and your device settings.

DouScan iOS 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月5日。本政策适用于 DouScan iOS 1.0.8 版本。

扫码、生成及本地记录

QR & Barcode Scanner: DouScan 在设备本地处理相机画面或用户选择的图片，识别二维码和条形码。生成内容、扫码历史、收藏和已保存批次在本地处理和保存，不自动上传给开发者。扫码和生成功能可离线使用。打开应用不会自动发送设备注册或应用打开遥测。

权限及系统操作

相机权限用于现场扫码。用户选择图片扫码、选择Logo或保存生成图片时使用相关照片功能。联系人与日历操作交由系统编辑器，由用户确认；旧版iOS可能请求日历权限，系统联系人建议可能涉及通讯录权限。用户可在iOS设置中管理权限。应用不会自动向开发者上传通讯录、日历或照片库。

用户主动选择的对外操作

用户确认打开网址后，浏览器或网页视图会联系该网站。搜索、地图和商品查询会先展示目标及内容并请求确认：所显示的扫码文本交给Google Search，位置内容交给Google Maps，商品条码交给Barcode Lookup。取消确认不会发起该查询。接收方依其自身隐私政策处理信息。

用户分享、导出CSV或导出备份时，所选内容会交给用户指定的应用或保存位置；该位置可能是第三方服务或云端文件夹。DouScan不提供开发者运营的扫码记录云同步。系统备份和恢复受用户的Apple设置影响，本政策不承诺禁用所有iOS系统备份。

可选意见反馈

用户主动提交反馈时，开发者服务器会收到反馈正文、类别、应用生成的安装标识及用户自愿填写的联系方式，用于排查问题和沟通。反馈通过HTTPS传输。反馈为可选功能，与本地扫码和生成分开。

后台在处理请求后不保留反馈正文及其关联的安装标识和联系方式，因此没有可供申请删除的后台反馈存档。请勿在反馈中填写密码或其他敏感信息。如有隐私问题，可通过下方邮箱联系开发者。

广告及本地删除

此iOS版本没有广告SDK或跨应用广告追踪。本地记录默认没有固定保留期限，保存在设备上，直到用户主动删除，或按用户启用的可选历史清理设置移除。用户可以在应用中删除本地历史和已保存记录。用户主动导出、分享的副本以及系统备份，分别受接收位置和设备设置管理。
